\section{Conclusions}
This paper reviewed the evidence behind TrackSeed, an AR system for setting out FS tracks with a live digital twin. The literature shows that AR placement reaches centimetre-to-decimetre accuracy over small areas, that phones are the least accurate devices, and that tracking error grows with the distance from the reference; no study has evaluated phone-based setout over a full track. These findings led to two-point marker alignment with re-alignment, WebSocket synchronisation with a resend queue, a live twin whose edits reach the phone as guidance, and on-device cone verification and car identification. The next steps are to implement AR alignment on a skidpad, measure the drift on the team's test site to set the marker spacing, and then validate placement accuracy, setup time, synchronisation latency and car identification on a full track.
